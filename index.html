I'm a PhD researcher at the Ernst Strüngmann Institute (ESI) & Goethe University Frankfurt. My dissertation is about wireless neural recording implants and markerless 3D pose estimation with multi-camera systems: an 8-camera Basler rig tracks a monkey with sub-millimetre accuracy (~95% less manual annotation)

Before that: an M.Sc. in Biomedical Engineering (University of Isfahan), where I modelled sodium excretion with GMDH + MLP networks and estimated force from surface EMG with deep learning; a research stint at the University of Isfahan on medical image segmentation (U-Net, IoU >96%) and EEG/ECG-based detection; a part-time Data Scientist role at DataCoLab London (OCR pipelines, face recognition >97%, multilingual NLP); and a B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering (Jundishapur University of Technology) with a thesis on non-invasive potassium estimation from ECG.

On the industry side I've shipped OCR, face-recognition and NLP systems at DataCoLab London and built scalable ML infrastructure — Docker, CI/CD, MLflow, AWS/GCP and Linux HPC clusters — plus mentored engineers and managed stakeholders across labs and startups. So I'm as comfortable architecting a platform or leading a team as I am training the models: senior engineer, solution architect, Biomedical Engineer or advisor, depending on what you need.

In my spare time I maintain open-source ML tools — fuzzyart (polynomial neural nets + MOPSO for small-n biomedical data). Both get live demos in the playground below.

fuzzyart — Fuzzy ARTMAP, live port

Port of my fuzzyart package (Carpenter et al. 1992): complement coding A=(u,1−u,v,1−v), choice Tj=|A∧Wj|+(1−α)(M−|Wj|) with α=0.01, match |A∧Wj|/M ≥ ρ, match-tracking (label conflict ⇒ ρ=match+|ε|, ε=−0.001) and learning W←β(A∧W)+(1−β)W, β=0.2. With 2 features M=4, so a point fully inside a box scores 0.5.

mopso — multi-objective PSO, live port

Port of my mopso (Coello Coello & Lechuga 2002): v = w·v + c1·r1·(pbest−x) + c2·r2·(leader−x), Pareto domination (all ≤, any <), hyper-grid archive, leader roulette exp(−β·n), pruning exp(−γ·n), inertia damping w ← 0.99·w each iteration.
